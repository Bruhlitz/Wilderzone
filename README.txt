WILDERZONE - WINDOWS
=====================

Run SkiForever.exe. Keyboard and mouse or a controller.
SPACE skis, RIGHT MOUSE jets and jumps, LEFT MOUSE fires, WASD steers, MOUSE looks, ESC pauses; all rebindable in OPTIONS.

PLAYING WITH FRIENDS (CAPTURE THE FLAG, RABBIT or TEAM DEATHMATCH, up to ten pilots; the one who hosts picks the mode and the map in the lobby):
  1. Send this whole folder to each friend (zip it). Everyone must run the same build.
  2. One of you: PLAY > MULTIPLAYER > HOST A MATCH. A code appears. Press COPY and send it to the others.
  3. The others: PLAY > MULTIPLAYER, press PASTE beside A FRIEND'S CODE, then JOIN A MATCH.
  4. Everyone appears in the lobby. Pick sides with SWITCH SIDES. The one who hosted presses START THE MATCH.
In a match: 1 2 3 pick a weapon, Q fires your core, G uses your accessory, Z throws the flag,
H uses your health kit, K respawns you, L opens your loadout, hold TAB for the scoreboard, M opens the map.
FLY IT ALONE flies the mode and map picked above it alone: against bots in RABBIT and TEAM DEATHMATCH (BOTS and SKILL beside it).
PLAY > SHOOTING RANGE is for trying the guns alone: targets that break and come back, and three pilots who never shoot.
The first time, Windows may ask to let the game through the firewall: allow it.
